**Romance Research** is a playful independent institute exploring love, attraction, compatibility, heartbreak, and modern dating behavior.

We publish essays, working papers, field observations, and speculative frameworks about human connection.

The project sits somewhere between a journal, a cultural lab, and a research experiment.

### Research topics

• attraction and chemistry  
• dating behavior and modern courtship  
• emotional uncertainty  
• compatibility and timing  
• situationships and ambiguity  
• heartbreak and recovery
