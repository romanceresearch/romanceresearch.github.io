A graph-theoretic analysis of elite mate search in Beijing, Shanghai, and Shenzhen using synthetic social networks and the BigCLAM community model.

**Key findings**

- Estimated probability ≈ **1.45%**
- Probability for 30+: **1.38%**
- Social network access increases probability **10×**
- Network position matters more than age.
